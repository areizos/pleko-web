Notion for business

Your whole business in one place. Set up properly.

Notion is a tool for keeping clients, projects, tasks and notes in one place, instead of scattered across Excel, WhatsApp and email. It is very flexible, and that is exactly why it is hard to get going. Here you can see what it can do and how it connects to what you already use.

Sound familiar?

  • Clients, quotes and tasks live in three different spreadsheets.
  • Follow-up happens on WhatsApp, and what was said a month ago is gone.
  • An important document is in some email, or maybe in a folder.
  • Decisions get lost: nobody remembers who agreed what, or when.

What Notion can do for you

Six common uses. You don't need all of them: we start with the one that hurts most.

Clients and contacts

One record per person or company, with their history, notes and what is left to do. A simple CRM, without the complications of a big one.

Projects and tasks

A board like a whiteboard: what is planned, what is under way and what is done. Every task has an owner and a date.

Your daily dashboard

A page that shows only what is yours: what is due today, this week and what is waiting for a reply. Everyone on the team gets their own.

Procedures

How each thing is done, written once. Nothing depends on anyone's memory, and a new starter knows where to begin.

Meeting notes

Each meeting has its own page, linked to the client and the project. What was said ends up where you will look for it.

Content calendar

What you publish, when, and what state it is in. You write it; Notion just helps you not lose the thread.

Notion, connected to what you already use.

On its own, Notion doesn't talk to your WhatsApp or your invoicing. For that I put a piece in the middle, a connector (n8n or Make), that carries information from one place to another. Tick what you use and see how it would connect.

    What nobody tells you

    Notion is a blank page, and that is both its strength and its trap.

    • Getting started is easy. Getting it right is another matter: templates almost never fit your business as they are.
    • It is very easy to build too much: fifteen tables nobody opens.
    • It doesn't talk to WhatsApp or your invoicing tool by itself. That takes a connector.
    • If the team doesn't open it from day one, it quietly dies.

    That is where I come in: I set it up with you, connect it to what you already use and teach you to run it yourself. That saves you weeks of trial and starting over.

    Questions

    Is Notion free?

    It has a free plan for individual use. As soon as several of you work together you need a paid plan, charged per person per month. Prices change, so before we start I tell you what it would be in your case.

    Does it replace my spreadsheet or my CRM?

    Sometimes yes, and sometimes there is no need. For a small business, Notion can be your CRM. But if what you have already works, I won't change it for the sake of it: in the Diagnostic I tell you honestly whether Notion adds anything.

    Where is my data?

    On the plans small businesses use, Notion stores data outside the EU; as far as I know, the option to store it in the EU is on their Enterprise plan. That is why I keep very sensitive data, such as health data, out of Notion, and I explain what goes in and what doesn't before we start.

    Does it work on my phone?

    Yes, there is an app for phone and for computer. It is great for looking things up and jotting things down; I do the building from a computer.

    If I stop using it one day, can I take my data?

    Yes. Notion lets you export your pages and tables, for example to CSV and Markdown. I also set it up in your own account, so it is yours from day one.
