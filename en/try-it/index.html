Connect the pieces

This is what an automation is: something comes in, AI processes it and it lands where it should, without anyone copying and pasting. Pick your business and one piece from each column, or press “Surprise me”.

    How it's built: In tools like n8n or Make, each piece is one step. These examples use made-up data; in your business they would be your real forms, email and apps. Greyed-out pieces don't fit what you picked before.

    How it's built: Rules that recognise Spanish ID numbers (checking the letter), IBANs, phone numbers, emails, cards (with their check digit) and common first names. It all happens in your browser. No detection is perfect, which is why a person always checks what matters.
