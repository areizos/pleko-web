Service · Chatbots

A chatbot that answers what people always ask, and passes on what it can’t.

I’ll explain what a chatbot can do for your business, what it can’t do and how one gets built. If you’re not sure you need one, we start there: we look at the questions that keep coming back and decide whether it’s worth it.

Sound familiar?

  • The same questions arrive on WhatsApp or your website: opening hours, prices, whether you do this or that.
  • You lose enquiries outside office hours because nobody replies until the next day.
  • Bookings go back and forth by message, and some get lost.
  • You don’t know if a chatbot would help, or whether it would end up answering things it shouldn’t.

What it can and can’t do

Includes

What it does well

Answers frequent questions at any hour, collects contact details, takes a booking request and passes the conversation to a person when needed.

Includes

What it shouldn’t do

Make up answers, set a price or a deadline on your behalf, or replace a person on a sensitive case. If it doesn’t know something, it says so and flags it.

Includes

What it needs to work

Clear information to work from: your frequent questions, your services, your terms. Without that, any chatbot will improvise.

Includes

Where it lives

On your website, in WhatsApp Business or in the channel where people already write to you. You don’t need to change tools to start.

What I don’t do

I don’t sell a chatbot that pretends to be a person.

A chatbot should say it’s an assistant. It shouldn’t pass itself off as you or your team, and your name, your tone and your clients’ details aren’t used to fool anyone. What it does is make the conversation easier, and make it clear when a person is on the other end.

How I work

  1. 01

    We talk

    Which questions you get, where they come in, and how many a day.

  2. 02

    We check if it’s worth it

    Before building anything, we decide whether a chatbot solves something real, or whether a saved answer is enough.

  3. 03

    I build and test it

    In your accounts, with real questions from your clients, before it talks to anyone.

  4. 04

    I leave it explained

    What it answers, what it doesn’t, how to correct it, and when to hand over to a person.

Questions

Can a chatbot quote my prices?

Only if they’re written down for it. If prices change, they need updating, which is why I leave it explained.

Is my data safe?

I work with European providers where possible, with the minimum access needed, and I explain everything before we start.

Does it need looking after?

Yes, now and then: prices, services and questions change. I leave it explained so you can do it yourself.
