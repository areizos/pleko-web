Service · Writing in your voice

Write more, and still sound like you.

I build automation and AI systems for people and businesses who have things to say and never find the time to write them. They help you brainstorm ideas, organise them and turn them into drafts of blog posts, newsletters or product descriptions. You pick the ideas. And you have the final word.

Sound familiar?

  • Your head is full of ideas and the blog hasn’t moved in months.
  • You’ve tried ChatGPT and the text sounds like anyone but you.
  • The newsletter goes out “when there’s time”, which is usually never.
  • Writing a product description or a long email eats your afternoon.

What I set up

Includes

Your voice guide

We put it together from what you’ve already written: your words, your tone, what you’d never say.

Includes

Someone to think with

An AI to bounce ideas off: tell it something in two sentences or a voice note, and it gives you angles, questions and an outline.

Includes

From outline to draft

A workflow that turns your notes into a first draft with your structure and your way of speaking, ready for you to make it yours.

Includes

Training

I teach you to use it and adjust it when your voice or your business changes.

What I don’t do

I don’t write a clone of you.

This isn’t about inventing someone who writes in your name, or filling the internet with empty text. Your ideas, experience and judgement aren’t automated. What can be automated is the heavy lifting: organising, structuring, tidying up. AI prepares. You think and decide.

How I work

  1. 01

    We talk

    What you want to write, who for, and what’s holding you back.

  2. 02

    We collect your voice

    Your texts, emails, posts. We build the guide from them.

  3. 03

    I set it up and we test it

    With two or three of your real pieces, until the draft sounds like you.

  4. 04

    I leave it explained

    A written guide and a session so you can use it day to day.

Questions

Does the AI write it, or do I?

Both. The AI prepares a draft from your ideas; you edit it and sign it.

Are my texts used to train the AI?

I set the tools up so they aren’t, wherever the provider allows it, and I explain it before we start.

Does it work in Spanish?

Yes, in English and Spanish.
