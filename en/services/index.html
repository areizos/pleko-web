Workflow automation

I turn manual, error-prone admin steps into processes that run themselves.

For example

Onboarding and invoicing without friction. When a client accepts a quote, their contact appears in your CRM, the draft invoice in Holded, their folder in Drive and your team gets a heads-up. In seconds, with no mistyped tax ID or amount.

Enquiries and sales follow-up. Every enquiry from your website, WhatsApp Business or portals like Idealista gets an instant reply, is logged and sets off automatic reminders, so no quote goes cold.

Month-end for your gestor. Invoices and receipts from email, WhatsApp and your phone are gathered into one folder with a summary sheet, so the end of the month isn't a scramble.

Voice notes that become action. You dictate after a visit or a call. The system turns it into a task, a note on the client and a draft follow-up email, ready to review.

AI-assisted systems

AI assistants that read, check your data and act, with a person on your team approving what matters.

For example

Supplier invoices without typing. An invoice arrives by email or as a PDF; the system reads the supplier, amount, VAT and date, logs it in your accounts or Excel, and asks you for one click to approve it. Same for contracts and delivery notes.

A chatbot that checks and reserves. An assistant connected to your inventory that compares products, answers the customer and reserves the one they want, at any hour.

Replies and drafts with your own judgement. An assistant connected to your history that instantly drafts replies to clients, proposals or reports in English or Spanish, ready to review and send.

Content in your voice. A system that helps you come up with topics and turn them into drafts of posts, articles, newsletters or product descriptions, built on what you've already written, and gets them ready to schedule. All in your own accounts. You pick the ideas and have the final word before anything goes out.

Tool integration

I connect the apps you already pay for so they talk to each other, without making you switch software.

For example

No more data islands. Your Excel sheets, web forms, WhatsApp, CRM and invoicing software joined into one flow, so nobody types the same data twice.

Your business, up to date. A clean dashboard with your real sales, hours worked and invoicing up to date, without losing Monday morning cross-checking five different files.

Documents filed in the right place. The quote someone emailed in March, the contract that came by WhatsApp, the invoice in your downloads folder, the receipt photo on your phone. The system picks them up where they land, reads what each one is, and files it in the right client folder in Drive, named the same way every time. You search once, not in five places.

One client record. A client writes on WhatsApp, calls from another number, and is already in your invoicing software under a slightly different name. The system matches them up. Their enquiries, quotes, invoices and notes sit on one record, and whoever picks up the phone sees the whole story.
