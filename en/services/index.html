Services and pricing

Clear quotes, no surprises.

You don't know what automating something costs or where to start, and hardly anyone gives you a price without a meeting first. Here they are. I always work in phases, with a clear quote agreed before we start.

  1. 01

    Operational Diagnosis

    €259 + VAT (deducted from the project)

    A joint working session (about 2 hours) where we review how you work today, spot the bottlenecks, decide what goes where, and you leave with a clear roadmap of what to automate first. The full amount is deducted if you hire a project within the following 30 days.

  2. 02

    First Thread (One automation)

    From €890 + VAT

    Ideal for solving your biggest bottleneck usually in one or two weeks. One specific process built in your own accounts, documented transparently so you understand how it works and know how to adapt it if your business changes.

  3. 03

    Your Connected Business

    From €2,490 + VAT

    For businesses that need their sales, bookings and invoices to talk to each other. I connect your inbound channels (website, WhatsApp, bookings) with your internal management and invoicing, so you stop moving data by hand from one screen to another.

  4. Ongoing Support (Optional)

    Although every system is delivered ready to run from day one, I offer optional monthly support for regular reviews, adjustments when things change and small ongoing improvements, for those who would rather hand over the oversight.
