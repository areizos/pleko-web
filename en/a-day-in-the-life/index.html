Example · A day in the life

A day in the life of an event organiser.

An event means hundreds of back-and-forth messages between suppliers, dates and invoices. Here’s the same work before and after it’s put in order. A simulation with a fictional person.

Manual actions in this example: 73After, only 8 checks are left

An action is anything you do by hand: open, copy, paste, type, send or check. Counted across the steps on this page.

Couple Restaurant Musician Florist Quote Booking Invoice You

  1. 1 · Enquiry

    A couple writes asking for an autumn wedding for 80 guests. The enquiry is saved with date, budget and requests.

    Checked by you: whether to take the job.

    4 actions1 check

  2. 2 · Suppliers

    One email each to the restaurant, the musician and the florist, with the same date and terms. Replies are gathered into one sheet.

    Checked by you: you confirm with each supplier.

    27 actions3 checks

  3. 3 · Quote

    A draft quote is built from the supplier replies.

    Checked by you: you adjust it and send it.

    11 actions1 check

  4. 4 · Booking

    When the couple accepts, the date is blocked and each supplier gets its confirmation.

    Checked by you: you sign off and confirm.

    15 actions1 check

  5. 5 · Invoice

    The deposit and balance invoices are prepared with the couple’s details.

    Checked by you: you check each one before it’s sent.

    16 actions2 checks
