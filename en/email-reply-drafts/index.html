Service · Email reply drafts

Start every reply halfway there, not from scratch.

I set up a system that prepares draft replies to the emails you get most often, based on your own texts and the way you answer. You check it, change what needs changing and send. So you never start an email with a blank page.

Sound familiar?

  • You write the same replies again and again, with small changes.
  • Every new email costs more than it should, even when it’s simple.
  • You spend the afternoon replying, and the important things get left until last.
  • Your tone changes from one day to the next, and clients notice.

What's included

Includes

Your saved replies

The questions that come up most, answered the way you’d answer them, grouped by topic: quotes, bookings, invoices, questions.

Includes

A draft for every email

You read the email that arrives, and the system prepares a first reply from what it already knows. You adjust it and send.

Includes

Your tone, still yours

It draws on what you’ve already written, so the draft sounds like you. And every reply goes through your hands.

Includes

What no one answers for you

Sensitive cases, complaints and agreements stay with you. The system flags any email that doesn’t fit the usual pattern.

What I don’t do

No automatic replies that nobody has read.

I don’t send emails in your name without you seeing them, and I don’t write generic replies that sound like a robot. A draft is a starting point. The final word, and the responsibility, stay with you.

How I work

  1. 01

    We talk

    Which emails you get, which ones repeat, and which take the most of your time.

  2. 02

    We collect your replies

    The ones you already send, so the draft sounds like you and not like a manual.

  4. 04

    How to add new replies, how to correct a draft, and when to stop using it.

Questions
