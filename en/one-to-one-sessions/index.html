Learn to work with AI, with me beside you.

Everyone is talking about AI and hardly anyone knows what to do with it in their own work. I work with you, live, on your own cases and your own accounts, so you leave knowing what to use, how and why. It isn’t a course or a custom project: it’s my time with you.

Why these sessions exist

There are courses everywhere, but most explain how AI works in general or show generic examples. Your day isn’t generic, and you don’t have time to try a hundred tools to find the one that fits. Several people have asked me for exactly this: someone to sit down with them and show them how to do it with their own cases. These sessions come from that.

Very capable people still do by hand what now takes minutes: moving data from one place to another, repeating the same steps every week. And with AI it’s the same: copy from one tool, paste into another, start again. It isn’t a lack of intelligence: this has changed so fast that hardly anyone has had time to catch up.

Who it’s for

Freelancers and self-employed

Your business is you, and the day only has so many hours. We work on quotes and proposals, following up with contacts, replying to clients, invoices and payments, and a monthly money overview. Also good text for your website and social media, and your tools connected so they talk to each other.

Lawyers, tax advisors and accountants

Lots of documents and little time. We look at how to summarise long contracts, compare clauses and know what to ask, research with sources you can check and build your own knowledge base. And we make clear which client data must never go into an AI tool.

Consultants and public-facing professionals

Your work is explaining well and arriving prepared. Well-structured presentations and reports, research and summaries of long documents, preparing for meetings and notes with action points at the end. And your tools connected so nothing gets lost between them.

Health, therapy and training

Your time is for people, not paperwork. Meeting notes, order in your email, files and calendar, text for your communication and, above all, real care with your clients’ or students’ data.

What we can work on pick what helps you

Clients and sales

So no client waits longer than they should and you don’t lose your afternoons writing.

  • Quotes and proposals: reusable templates and first drafts with AI that you review and send.
  • Following up with contacts: emails and short sequences in your voice, and a simple way to see who is waiting for what.
  • Replying to client emails and WhatsApp in your tone, faster.
  • Preparing for a call: researching the person or company in ten minutes.
  • Meeting notes: summaries and action points ready to send.

Money and admin

So you know what comes in, what goes out and what’s due each month, without chasing paper.

  • Invoicing: clean templates, supplier invoices read into a spreadsheet and polite payment reminders.
  • A monthly money overview and a quarterly checklist of what to have ready (organisation, not tax advice).
  • Contracts and long documents: summarise, compare clauses and spot what to ask. A person or lawyer always signs.
  • Order in your email, files and calendar: labels, folders and search that works.

Content and presence

So what you publish sounds like you and takes less time.

  • Posts, newsletter and LinkedIn in your voice, without sounding like AI.
  • Presentations and reports: structure, slides, speaker notes and clearer visuals.
  • Website text, Google Business Profile and basic SEO.

Knowledge and research

So you can quickly find what you know and check what you read.

  • Research with sources you can check, and summaries of long PDFs and reports.
  • A personal knowledge base: your notes, templates and instructions in one searchable place.
  • Translation and tone adaptation between Spanish, English and other languages.

Tools and safety

To use AI with judgement and with your data protected.

  • Choosing and setting up the right AI tools for you: account, settings, what to pay for and what not.
  • Your own library of reusable instructions, so you don’t start from zero each time.
  • Your tools connected: your email, calendar, notes and invoices talking to each other. We set it up together, step by step, and you understand how it works.
  • Privacy: what never goes into an AI tool, client confidentiality and what the tools do with your data.

What it is and isn’t

We work together so you gain ease and order.

It isn’t a fixed project and I don’t promise a finished system: we go as far as the hours allow. If you later want something bigger built for you, that is a different service, on services and pricing. AI is a support for thinking and preparing; the decisions and the signature are yours.

Privacy

Before we start we agree what information can be used and what can’t. Your clients’ data doesn’t go into any AI tool unless we’ve checked it together.
