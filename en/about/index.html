About

Why I started pleko.

For most of my career, the best tools belonged to companies with big budgets and teams of developers. That's no longer true. AI puts that kind of leverage within reach of anyone willing to learn.

I've seen it from the inside: 15+ years in digital marketing, in London and Barcelona, always the one looking for a simpler way to do things. pleko is how I help people and companies make that jump, calmly and honestly, by connecting the tools you already use rather than selling you new ones. You work directly with me, and my aim is simple: tools that work for you, and a team that knows how to keep using them.

  • 15+ years in digital marketing
  • 2 working languages: Spanish and English
  • 1 person from start to finish: me
πλέκω plé·ko · to weave

In ancient Greek, pleko means "to weave" or "to braid". It's the root of words like complex and plexus: intertwining different things until they form something whole. That's what I do with your tools.
