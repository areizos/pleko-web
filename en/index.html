05 — AI with judgement

Useful, safe and under your control.

AI is powerful, but it isn't infallible. So every flow comes with its own brakes.

  • A person checks what matters.

    Nothing that reaches a customer or costs money goes out without someone seeing it.

  • AI only gets access to what it needs.

    Minimum permissions, keys locked away and your data kept out of places it shouldn't be.

  • Everything is logged and explained.

    What each flow did and when, documented in your accounts.

How I look after security →

The hard part isn't any one piece. It's knowing how they fit.

Behind every automation there are lots of pieces. You don't need to know what each one is: that's my job. But if you're curious, tap any of them.

  • Generative AI / LLM

    The “brain” that reads and writes text, like ChatGPT or Claude.

  • Large and small models

    Large ones reason better; small ones are fast and cheap. Efficient means using each where it fits.

  • Prompt

    The instructions you give the AI. Written well, they change everything.

  • API

    The door two programs use to talk to each other.

  • Webhook

    An automatic “something just happened” ping.

  • JSON

    The format data travels in between apps.

  • MCP

    A standard plug that connects AI to your tools.

  • RAG

    Letting AI answer from your documents, not from memory.

  • AI agent

    AI that doesn't just answer, but takes steps and uses tools.

  • Human-in-the-loop

    The point where a person checks before things move on.

  • Evals

    Tests that check the AI answers well, before and after every change.

  • Prompt injection

    Hidden instructions in an email or web page that try to trick the AI.

  • Least privilege

    Each piece gets only the access it needs, nothing more.

07 — FAQ

What people usually ask before the first call.

Can't see your question? Email me at alex@pleko.es and I'll answer it myself.

What exactly does pleko do?

I help you get rid of manual, repetitive work. I connect the tools you already use, automate the steps you do by hand today and add AI only where it really saves time. You end up with clear workflows that run on their own, and you understand how they work.

Do I have to change the tools I use?

Almost never. I start with what you already have (Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Excel, your CRM) and get them talking to each other. I only suggest something new if it really saves you work.

We tried AI and it didn't stick. What's different?

It usually fails because AI is tried “in general” rather than inside a specific everyday task. I start with one real process, untangle it, then automate it. Automating a tangled process just tangles it faster.

What about my data?

Everything lives in your accounts, documented and yours. I use the minimum permissions needed, follow GDPR and can sign an NDA if you need one.

What happens when you're done?

Everything is documented and lives in your accounts. I leave you training materials and teach you or your team how to use the workflows and change them when needed.

Do you work in person with companies in Barcelona?

Yes, it's what I enjoy most. I live in Barcelona and prefer to sit with your team and see how you really work. For follow-ups, or with clients further away, I also work by video call, in English or Spanish.
