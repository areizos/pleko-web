02 — Services

I take the tangled threads of how your business runs and weave them into a clear system.

Three ways I can help, which usually go together. I start with the bottleneck that costs you the most time or margin today.

Workflow automation

I turn manual, error-prone admin steps into processes that run themselves.

For example

Onboarding and invoicing without friction. When a client accepts a quote, their contact appears in your CRM, the draft invoice in Holded, their folder in Drive and your team gets a heads-up. In seconds, with no mistyped tax ID or amount.

Enquiries and sales follow-up. Every enquiry from your website, WhatsApp Business or portals like Idealista gets an instant reply, is logged and sets off automatic reminders, so no quote goes cold.

AI-assisted systems

I add AI where it genuinely speeds up work and cuts costs, always with your team in control.

For example

Reading invoices and documents. A system that reads supplier invoices, contracts or delivery notes in PDF, pulls out the key data and puts it in your accounts or Excel, so a person only has to check it with one click.

Replies and drafts with your own judgement. An assistant connected to your history that instantly drafts replies to clients, proposals or reports in English or Spanish, ready to review and send.

Tool integration

I connect the apps you already pay for so they talk to each other, without making you switch software.

For example

No more data islands. Your Excel sheets, web forms, WhatsApp, CRM and invoicing software joined into one flow, so nobody types the same data twice.

Your business, up to date. A clean dashboard with your real sales, hours worked and invoicing up to date, without losing Monday morning cross-checking five different files.

04 — FAQ

What people usually ask before the first call.

Can't see your question? Email me at alex@pleko.es and I'll answer it myself.

What exactly does pleko do?

I help you get rid of manual, repetitive work. I connect the tools you already use, automate the steps you do by hand today and add AI only where it really saves time. You end up with clear workflows that run on their own, and you understand how they work.

Do I have to change the tools I use?

Almost never. I start with what you already have (Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Excel, your CRM) and get them talking to each other. I only suggest something new if it really saves you work.

We tried AI and it didn't stick. What's different?

It usually fails because AI is tried “in general” rather than inside a specific everyday task. I start with one real process, untangle it, then automate it. Automating a tangled process just tangles it faster.

What about my data?

Everything lives in your accounts, documented and yours. I use the minimum permissions needed, follow GDPR and can sign an NDA if you need one.

What happens when you're done?

Everything is documented and lives in your accounts. I leave you training materials and teach you or your team how to use the workflows and change them when needed.

Do you work in person with companies in Barcelona?

Yes, it's what I enjoy most. I live in Barcelona and prefer to sit with your team and see how you really work. For follow-ups, or with clients further away, I also work by video call, in English or Spanish.
