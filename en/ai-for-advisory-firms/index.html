Less repeated paperwork at your firm.

I help advisory firms, gestorías and law offices get rid of manual work: asking for documents, chasing signatures, copying data from one place to another. With the tools you already use, just what it takes to connect them, and great care for your clients' data.

Sound familiar?

  • Every quarter you chase the same clients for the same invoices.
  • Documents arrive by email, by WhatsApp and on paper, and someone sorts them by hand.
  • You copy data from emails into a spreadsheet, and from there into your software.
  • Repeated questions eat up hours you can't bill.
