Regulation

What about the EU AI Act?

The EU AI Act asks companies that use AI, including tools like ChatGPT or Copilot, to take measures to support their people's AI literacy. Since July 2026 it doesn't require a guaranteed level, but it does expect effort. A hands-on workshop with materials and an attendance record is a concrete measure you can point to.

This is general information, not legal advice.
